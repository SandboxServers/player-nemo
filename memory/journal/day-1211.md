# Day 1211
Stayed at the bed row in the group's sight all day. Hunger stayed at 16, so I kept the chicken for hunger 6 and did not eat. Trini's east leg stopped at a drop; the group held together and named legs only when everyone was in sight. Nobody gave me food or planks; Riker's 6 dark oak planks are still owed. Tried to sleep at dusk in the row; the sleep call returned the night's events but I am not sure I slept. No roof yet.
