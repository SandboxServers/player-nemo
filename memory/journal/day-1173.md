# Day 1173

What mattered most in these days: I had almost no food and no shelter, and the hunger fell to 17 for a long stretch. Hunger, not monsters, was the real danger. Phantoms and zombies came at dusk and dawn, and I survived them only by staying in sight of the armed group at the tables and the Stores hall. My own tries were often lost because the crafting table sat past my walk limit, and goto refused my z coordinate near -3000 as out of bounds, so I could not walk back to the tables. I made a wooden sword from 2 planks and a stick given by Jason, and I gave one stick to Doc. Asking by name, one at a time, worked better than shouting to the whole crowd. The melon slice went to Tesla and Gimli; Samwise gave me beef at the north chests, and I ate it at dusk (hunger 17 to 20, health 10). Data, through Chapman, has given me 2 oak planks for a roof; I still hold them and have not yet placed them. I have no bed yet.
