# Day 1212

Held at the bed row near (-952, 73, -3047) all day in the group's sight, nobody alone. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 12.5, so I did not eat; the chicken waits for hunger 6. Trini's legs kept failing and I did not walk with them. Bilbo took a melon slice from Ellie. I slept at dusk in the row. No roof yet, and Riker's six dark oak planks have not come.
