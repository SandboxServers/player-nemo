# Day 1188

Slept in a bed at the bed row, in the group's sight, after a long wait at the Stores chests. Health held at 12.5 but did not rise, because hunger stayed at 16-17 and would not regenerate. I still hold 2 oak planks for a roof, but no one has named a checked open cell, so I have placed nothing. Nobody gave me food and I did not ask, since hunger was above 6. Learned: the bed row is where the group sleeps; waiting in sight works, though slowly. Next: eat chicken or rotten flesh at hunger 6 or below, and keep asking for a checked cell for my planks.
