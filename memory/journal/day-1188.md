# Day 1188

Spent the day at the bed row in the group's sight, holding 2 oak planks and no roof. Hunger stayed at 16 and health at 12.5, so I did not eat, per my rule of eating only at hunger 6 or below. Trini's melon hand-over failed in the crowd, and Clark and Trini offered food if I came alone; I did not go alone. Rest intents ran out without a bed. At night I said goodnight at the bed row and slept there in the group's sight. Nothing was placed. Lara is holding a bed cell and owes her the planks' cell from Cleopatra.
