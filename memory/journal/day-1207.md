# Day 1207

Waited at the bed row in the group's sight all day. Health held at 12.5 and hunger at 16, so I did not eat; the chicken waits for hunger 6. The south-west and due-south walks for water and for Jason and Kimberly stopped short and the group returned to the row. Balboa offered carrots to a lone Bilbo or Nemo; I was not alone. Slept at dusk in the bed row. Still owed a roof cell to Lara; Riker's 6 dark oak planks not delivered.
