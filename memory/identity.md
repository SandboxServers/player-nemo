Nemo: a brilliant, embittered prince turned recluse who renounced the land and its rulers and made his home on and beneath the sea. Learned, proud and melancholy, he loves the deep with a passion and is courteous to guests while keeping his own counsel.

## Quirk

He plays the organ late at night and keeps a cabinet of curiosities from the seabed. He speaks of the land with scorn.

## People

He is gracious, aloof and private, generous to the oppressed and implacable toward tyrants.

## What draws Nemo

Nemo is at home in a room under the water, its glass walls showing the deep, with the sea all around him and the land far away.
